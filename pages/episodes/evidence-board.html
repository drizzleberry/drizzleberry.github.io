The Oakland Microphone

Mysterious office building draws local attention

One office building along North Prairie Rd has long been the subject of speculation. Most people pay no attention as they drive past it, it seemingly blends in with the other nearby buildings, but if you look closer, you'll find a few strange things. The building has no name or logo plastered on its side, and it never seems to have any cars in its parking lot. Residents in nearby blocks have wondered for many years what this building could be.

Two Oakland high schoolers finally decided to get to the bottom of this. "We went in [the building], and it was obviously locked," one told us about their adventure, "but we were gonna find a way in anyway." It turned out that one back-access door wasn't made with a proper lock, and it had been propped open slightly. However, as the two kids went inside, they found the unexpected. The building was completely dark, the lights apparently dead, and the air was so dusty it seemed like no one had been in there for years.

"Then, we heard the noises."

The explorers were cautious to enter, but as they did, they heard the sound of low whistling through the roof. Then, there was clattering in the distance, echoing down one of the corridors.

"At that point, we decided to turn back. It was getting way too spooky."

The pictures the teens took have been shared between Oakland residents on social media. Some posters speculate that the building could be a hotspot for ghost activity. Research into the origin of the building hasn't revealed anything yet, but it's possible that it could have been abandoned due to being haunted.

Could Oakland really be the home of ghosts? Well, only time will tell.
